# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, founders, and prospective product-consulting clients reviewing Madhur Jain's experience, proof of work, and ways to get in touch. Mobile visitors need to understand his product scope quickly and be able to explore evidence without treating the site like a resume document.

## Product Purpose

Present Madhur Jain as a product manager working across AI product systems, consumer growth, conversion, monetization, and operations. The portfolio should make his professional experience, measurable outcomes, capabilities, and personal product build credible and easy to evaluate.

## Positioning

A product manager portfolio that connects consumer-growth ownership and AI product management with practical builder proof, rather than presenting generic skills or a SaaS product landing page.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through a personal domain, search, a LinkedIn profile, or a direct shared link. They need a rapid first impression, clear proof by company and product context, detailed case-study routes, a resume download, social profiles, and direct contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The project is a Next.js App Router website. It must work on mobile and desktop, respect reduced-motion preferences, retain resume and contact actions, and keep professional facts grounded in the supplied resume and portfolio data. Mobile phone data is accessed through an API route. Production remains separate from the preview branch until the user approves it.

## Brand Commitments

The site uses Madhur Jain's custom M mark, a professional editorial voice, and references to Skiper UI, 21st.dev templates, and product-manager portfolio examples as inspiration. It must not look like a SaaS product or invent traction claims for Coach.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume-backed professional experience at PRISM (Parent Company of OYO), SplashLearn, and Honasa.
- Resume-backed metrics and case studies in `src/data/portfolio.ts`.
- Coach is a personal build across web, iOS, and Android, with current screenshots and a product walkthrough video.
- LinkedIn, GitHub, resume, email, and an API-backed mobile call action are available.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the work and the judgment behind it.
- Make outcome context understandable, not decorative.
- Keep professional experience distinct from personal product building.
- Make mobile feel authored for a phone, not compressed from desktop.
- Prefer real proof and precise language over generic portfolio claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, clear focus treatment, touch-friendly controls, sufficient color contrast, readable body text, and reduced-motion support.
